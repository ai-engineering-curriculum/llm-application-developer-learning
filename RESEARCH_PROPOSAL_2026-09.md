The curriculum's continuity bias — 'prefer citing existing content over proposing new content' — is the correct decision. Watch list for 2026-10: MCP tool-server development (may clear threshold in the Agentic AI Developer pool first), computer-use / browser agents, and voice / multimodal agents.
